# Cohort Privacy Notice — Beta Draft

> **DRAFT — NOT A COMPLETE PRIVACY POLICY.** This temporary notice describes the categories of data that may be processed by the beta. It must be completed and reviewed before production or paid launch.

Last updated: 22 November 2026

## 1. Data that may be processed

Depending on how you use Cohort, the service may process:
- Account and authentication information, such as an email address and identity-provider identifiers.
- Workspace information, including workspace names, membership, agent identifiers, branches, shared-state values, leases, and coordination events.
- Technical information needed to operate and secure the service, such as request metadata, error reports, and service logs.

Do not send credentials, secrets, or sensitive personal information unless you have assessed that the deployment is appropriate for that data.

## 2. Why data is processed

Data is processed to authenticate users, provide workspace and coordination features, troubleshoot failures, maintain service security, and improve reliability.

## 3. Hosting and service providers

Cohort relies on third-party infrastructure and identity services to operate. The exact provider list, processing locations, retention periods, and contractual roles must be documented here before this notice is treated as final.

## 4. Retention and deletion

Retention and deletion depend on the deployment and its underlying storage and hosting configuration. A definitive retention schedule and account/workspace deletion process must be published before production launch.

## 5. Sharing and model training

This draft does not make a complete representation about subprocessors, data transfers, or model-provider processing. Those details must be confirmed and documented before production launch. Do not treat this draft as a substitute for a deployment-specific data-processing review.
